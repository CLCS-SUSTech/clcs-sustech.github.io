Machine-generated text contains signals at statistical, spectral, and language-use levels. This project studies how stable those signals remain across models, tasks, and sampling methods.

## Detection and Evaluation

Beyond determining whether text is machine-generated, we investigate the relationship between automatic metrics and human judgments of quality.

## Methods

Our current methods combine frequency-domain analysis, statistical modeling, and psycholinguistic features, with an emphasis on robustness across domains.
