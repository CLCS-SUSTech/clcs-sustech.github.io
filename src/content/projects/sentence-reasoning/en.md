This project examines how language models organize intermediate reasoning. We study sentence boundaries, thought structures, and generation rhythms as factors that shape capability and interpretability.

## Research Questions

We ask when explicit linguistic structure helps models form more stable reasoning paths, and whether these gains generalize across tasks and model scales.

## Current Work

Our current work covers structured reasoning methods, capability-boundary analysis, and systematic comparisons of generation strategies.
