We are delighted to share that a paper from the lab has been accepted to COLM 2026.

**Uniform Information Density-Based Preference Optimization**

Congratulations to Yongyuan Li, the first author! The work uses the psycholinguistic phenomenon of uniform information density as an implicit reward for preference optimization, connecting language-model alignment with insights from information theory and psycholinguistics.
